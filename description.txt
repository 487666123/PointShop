最新版支持联机
---------------------------
一个用于兑换稀有物品的商店

    - 修复无法创建人物的的BUG

    - 玩家击杀敌怪会奖励玩家积分，根据怪物稀有度加分
     （稀有度可以在图鉴中查看）

    - 积分会添加到玩家所在环境的积分里

    - 屏幕上方积分统计和加分提示可以在设置中关闭

    - 商店面板开关可以在“控制”内设置快捷键

    - 可以在设置中调整商品的价格倍率

The latest version supports online
---------------------------
A shop to exchange rare items
    
    Players get bonus points upon defeating enemies, depend on the enemies's rarity
    (You may check rarity in the Beastiary)
    Points awarded will be added into biome which is where the player is

    Point statistic at the top of screen and notice for getting points can be disabled in config

    Bind a hotkey for Point Shop's panel in Controls
